About ClipForge Studio

ClipForge Studio is a browser-based creative workspace for making short-form videos, trailers, highlights, montages, captions and detailed edits.

ClipForge Studio is developed by Vexolune Technologies. Our goal is to make practical video-creation tools accessible through a simple workspace, with local browser processing wherever the workflow supports it.

Some optional AI-powered features use external AI services for tasks such as audio transcription and moment analysis. Please review our Privacy Policy to understand how those features handle information.
