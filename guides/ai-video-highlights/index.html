How to Review AI-Selected Video Highlights Before Publishing

Learn to choose a highlight duration, review AI-selected scenes, select music and check the final video before posting.

Try this workflow: Open Highlights Studio in ClipForge Studio. You can explore the controls before starting with a short test clip.

Define what the reel should communicate

Highlights are not simply the loudest moments. Think about whether the audience needs to see a sports recap, a conference summary, a short drama teaser or an event aftermovie. A clear goal helps you decide whether short AI-selected scenes form a coherent result.

Understand how selection works

In ClipForge Highlights Studio, audio transcription and AI analysis help select moments from a longer source. The results are suggestions, not a fact check or guarantee of a complete story. Dialogue that sounds dramatic may depend on missing context, so watch the generated scene plan and the finished montage critically.

Set a practical target duration

Choose the final highlights duration before running analysis. A longer target is not always better; a small set of meaningful scenes can be easier to follow. If the tool cannot find enough suitable moments to fill the requested time, the result may be shorter. Do not stretch weak moments simply to fill an exact target.

Consider output framing and audio

Choose the ratio for the platform before export. The Highlights workflow mutes the original source audio in the final reel and uses the selected generated music mood or an uploaded music track, with optional transitions or sound effects. This is important if a spoken sentence matters: the finished soundtrack will not preserve the original dialogue.

Check the result before uploading

Play the final reel in full. Look for sudden cutoffs, repeated or confusing scenes, mismatched aspect ratio, music that overwhelms the images and missing context. Confirm the exported duration in the target publishing app. Keep the original recording so you can re-edit if the selections misrepresent an event.

Respect source rights and privacy

Use footage and music you have permission to publish. Some AI features transmit audio or transcript data to services for analysis, so avoid sending sensitive source material without reviewing the product's privacy information first.

Quick quality checklist

  • AI-selected does not mean editorially final.
  • Highlights Studio mutes original video audio in the output.
  • Check clip context, framing and music before publishing.
