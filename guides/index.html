Choose a workflow for your project

If you already have a long recording, begin with the Video to Shorts workflow and review each suggested moment before export. For subtitles, the Captions Studio includes language selection and reusable caption looks. You can also explore the other ClipForge tools for trailers, montages, highlights and editing.

What we recommend testing first

Try a short sample from your own footage. Check whether it imports, whether the preview keeps sound and framing synchronized, whether Urdu or Hindi text is legible, and whether the downloaded file plays through in the app where you intend to publish it. Browser codec and hardware support vary. Keep your original media and a backup of finished exports.
